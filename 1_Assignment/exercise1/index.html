Albert Einstein

About Albert Einstein

Albert Einstein lived from 1879 to 1955. He was a physicist who is famous for his theory of relativity. He also worked on many other ideas in physics and won the Nobel Prize in 1921.

1879 - 1955

Key Events

  1. 1879 - Born in Ulm, Germany
  2. 1905 - Published his theory of special relativity
  3. 1915 - Published his theory of general relativity
  4. 1921 - Won the Nobel Prize in Physics
  5. 1933 - Moved to the United States
  6. 1955 - Died in Princeton, New Jersey

Key Contributions

  • Theory of relativity
  • E = mc^2 formula
  • Photoelectric effect
  • Contributions to quantum theory
